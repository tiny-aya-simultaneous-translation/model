## The data-efficiency finding (the spine of every deliverable)

Emergence order: **language identity (early) → text translation (strong) → audio
synthesis (next frontier).**

**How much training:** **2.07 epochs** over the 1,178,302-row train split
(≈**2.9 epochs** over the 840,426 unique clips), **2.44M samples, 6.59B tokens,
76,250 steps** on TPU v6e-16 (global batch 32).

**W&B teacher-forced emergence (run `xzcb60bl`):**

| metric | step 250 | 6k | 24k | 60k | 76k | first-crossing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| val text-token acc | 25.6% | 77.5% | 89.3% | 94.1% | **96.6%** | ≥50% @750, ≥90% @27.5k, ≥95% @66.75k |
| val cb0 (semantic) acc | 10.4% | 34.5% | 38.3% | 40.2% | **41.5%** | ≥40% @51.75k, then **plateaus** (frozen-depth-decoder ceiling) |
| val composite | 6.72 | 3.94 | 3.07 | 2.92 | **2.82** | monotonic |

**End-task `@best`=76,000 (free-run, greedy, 500-row `v03-val-500`):**

- free-run **text** chrF++ ~**25.7 / 25.1** (hi→tr / tr→hi) — the inner-monologue *translates*
- **ASR-chrF++** 3.7 / 9.6 (topline 92 / 87) — audio not yet ASR-intelligible
- **BLASER-QE 2.5/5** (ASR-free SONAR speech-semantic) — **higher than ASR/GEMBA imply**: the audio carries real, weak translation signal ASR can't transcribe
- DNSMOS Δ(gen−GT) −1.34; **GEMBA** adequacy ~1.1/5; **RTF 0.95**
- **Checkpoint selection:** `@best` ≈ `@final` (paired bootstrap p=0.19–0.26, NS); **LAWA gives no gain** (sig. worse tr→hi p=0.006) → **`@best` is the release checkpoint**.
- **FLEURS** (acoustic-shift): text collapses to ~8 → v0.3 is **distribution-bound**.

**Honest-positive one-liner:** *the full-corpus run shows the model learns the
translation MAPPING (text inner-monologue, 96.6% TF / ~25 chrF++) and genuine
speech-semantic signal (BLASER-QE 2.5) within ~3 epochs — intelligible audio
SYNTHESIS is the remaining, quantified frontier, bounded by the frozen depth
decoder, not the translation understanding.*
